About the operator

I'm a Forward Deployed Engineer and founder who ships machine learning into production — not slides about it.

I founded Dukamath, where I embed directly with retail customers and design, build, and deploy cloud-native point-of-sale systems for distributed retail networks. The platform runs in Python and SQL over large-scale real-time transaction data, with repeatable GCP environments provisioned through Terraform and containerized microservices shipped with Docker, automated on Linux and Bash. Every customer gets a system shaped to their actual workflow, not a generic product.

Before Dukamath I spent three years as a freelance ML consultant for e-commerce companies and startups — scoping to production, building the data pipelines that prepare training data, and applying NLP, computer vision, and time-series analysis while embedded inside client engineering teams. Earlier I authored 150+ journal articles, technical reports and whitepapers in quantitative and STEM disciplines (IEEE/APA), analyzing empirical data with SPSS, R and Python — so the research discipline behind the models is real, not a line on a CV.

Off the clock I build ML for the love of it: a character-level Swahili language model trained from scratch in PyTorch, stock-market predictors pairing ARIMA with neural nets and a reinforcement-learning trader, U-Net segmentation on satellite imagery, and convolutional networks written from the ground up in NumPy. ML is life — literally the bio on my GitHub.
